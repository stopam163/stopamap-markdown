# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in Stopamap.Markdown to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and specific.
- Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction.
- Give newcomers room to ask questions.
- Keep discussions in English or Chinese, and do not mock either language.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Insults, threats, sexualized attention, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption, spam, or bad-faith arguing.
- Plagiarizing code or violating a license.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, reject pull requests, or temporarily/permanently ban contributors. Report conduct issues privately to the repository owner. Reports will be handled confidentially.

## 中文摘要

参与本项目请保持尊重、对事不对人；禁止人身攻击、歧视、骚扰、泄露隐私和恶意破坏讨论。维护者可删除不当内容、关闭 Issue/PR，必要时限制或永久禁止参与。举报请私下联系仓库所有者，我们会保密处理。

This policy is adapted from the spirit of Contributor Covenant 2.1.
